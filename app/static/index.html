Every buy must clear the Research Desk. Before a position is opened the bot writes a full holistic report — thesis, valuation, fundamentals, revenue streams, confirmed & pending catalysts, and a recalculated forward-earnings view — then re-digests it into a research score. That score is combined with the engine buy-score into a Conviction Index; only a high-enough Index confirms the trade, and it also sizes it.
