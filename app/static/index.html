Research — Investment Theses研究 — 投资论文

Every buy must clear the Research Desk. Before a position is opened the bot writes a full holistic report — thesis, valuation, fundamentals, revenue streams, confirmed & pending catalysts, and a recalculated forward-earnings view — then re-digests it into a research score. That score is combined with the engine buy-score into a Conviction Index; only a high-enough Index confirms the trade, and it also sizes it. Click View thesis for the full report behind each decision. 每一笔买入都必须通过研究台。在建仓之前，机器人会撰写完整的整体研究报告——论点、估值、基本面、收入结构、已确认与待定催化剂，以及重新测算的前瞻盈利——再将其消化为研究评分。该评分与引擎买入分合成信念指数；只有指数足够高才确认交易，并据此决定仓位大小。点击查看论文查看每个决策背后的完整报告。
